01 / The daily chore

You shouldn't have to run your own home.

Every day is a sequence of small management tasks. Turning fans on and off. Checking whether the geyser was left running. Switching off lights burning in empty rooms. Coming back to a stuffy house.

Technology was supposed to help. Mostly it handed us one more app to manage, one more remote to find, one more command to remember.

02 / The idea

A home that looks after itself.

Switchless learns the rhythm of your days and keeps each room comfortable on its own — the right temperature, the right light, at the right time.

Already comfortable.

Your home adjusts itself through the day — reading the hour, the weather and which rooms are being used, and getting a space ready before you walk into it.

Nothing to learn.

No app to master, no hub to configure, nothing to say out loud. Every switch in your home still works exactly as it always has. Switchless only handles what you would rather not think about.

05 / Why you can trust it

Your switches still work.

Exactly as before. Every plate on every wall keeps doing what it always did — and the room answers to it first.

Nothing to learn.

No app to master, no commands to memorise, no settings to keep tuning. If you never open anything, the home still runs.

Works in a power cut.

And when the internet is down. The home keeps its own rhythm locally, so comfort does not depend on a connection somewhere else.
